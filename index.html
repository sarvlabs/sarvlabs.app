What we stand for

Four promises, rooted in our name.

sarv · the evergreen

Made with care, made to last

We would rather build one app that is a joy to open every morning, and keep improving it for years, than ship ten and walk away.

sarv · the upright

Honest by default

We say plainly what our apps do and what they collect. No dark patterns, no hidden catches, and we never sell anyone’s data.

sarva · whole and well

For the whole person

We design for wellbeing, not screen time. Progress is shown honestly, and hard days are forgiven, never punished.

sarva · for all

Open to everyone

Anyone should be able to begin. Our apps are accessible from day one and genuinely useful for free.

What’s in a name

Two old words, one small studio.

sarvسرو

Persian for the cypress: evergreen, upright, and called “the free tree” by the poet Saadi.

sarvaसर्व

Sanskrit for all and whole, from the same ancient root as the Latin word for safe and well.
