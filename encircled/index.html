Share your bookshelf with your circle.

How it works

Good books are meant to travel.

Add your shelf

Put the books you own into Encircled, so your circle can see what you have.

Build your circle

Bring in the friends and family you would happily lend a book to.

Lend and borrow

Find a book someone in your circle owns, ask to borrow it, and pass it on when you are done.

The best book recommendations come from the people who know you. Encircled puts their shelves within reach.
