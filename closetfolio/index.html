Closetfolio

Everything you love, in one calm, private place.

Photograph the clothes and accessories you own, and they become a clean, shop-style closet you can search. Getting dressed feels easier, and what you have feels good again.

Download on theApp Store Get it onGoogle Play

Closetfolio is opening to new members in stages. Request an invite in the app or at closetfolio.app.

How it works

Photograph it once. Find it any time.

Add a whole pile

Take a photo, or pick up to 50 from your phone at once. If one photo holds several pieces, each gets its own page.

Your photo in, a clean piece out

The background comes off, so every piece sits clean and easy to see. With Closet AI on, you can get an enhanced image too. Your original photo is always kept.

Tap a color, see every shade

Search by brand, color or tag, or filter by category, season and occasion. Your Lookbook keeps a dated record of what you wore.

Inside the app

Every piece you own, beautifully in one place.

Closetfolio exists for one reason: to help you feel good in the clothes you already own.
