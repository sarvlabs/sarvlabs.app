Privacy

Steadfast privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · SARV Labs LLC, Austin, Texas

Steadfast is made by SARV Labs LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. In short: we collect what the app needs to track your challenges, we never sell it, and you can delete it at any time.

What we collect

Account. When you sign in with Apple or with an email code, we receive an account identifier and an email address, which may be Apple’s private relay address.

Your challenges. The challenges you join, the tasks you log and when, skips, day notes, and your optional daily mood check.

Photos. Progress and meal photos you choose to take in the app.

Health data. Steadfast can read sleep, workouts and walking or running distance from Apple Health (Health Connect on Android), only if you turn it on. It never writes to Health. When a workout or night’s sleep completes one of your tasks, Steadfast saves only what that task needs: the activity type, its duration or distance, when it happened, and an identifier so the same workout isn’t counted twice. Everything else stays in Apple Health on your phone. Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shown to your partners; they see only whether a task was done.

Location. Location is optional. While the app is open, Steadfast uses your approximate location to show local weather and sunrise and sunset times. On iPhone your location goes to Apple’s WeatherKit; on Android it is rounded to about 10 km on your phone before it goes to our weather service, which keeps it in memory for no more than 10 minutes. Steadfast never saves your location to your account.

How we use it

Only to run Steadfast for you: keeping your days, showing your progress, sending the reminders you turn on, and answering you when you contact support. We do not use your data for advertising, and Health data is never used for marketing or shared with data brokers.

Where it is kept

Your account and challenge data are stored with Supabase, our database provider, on Amazon Web Services servers in Northern California, United States. Access is limited to your own account, and photos are kept in private storage.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run the app (Supabase for storage; Apple for sign-in, weather and payments on iPhone; Google Play for payments on Android; and our weather service, which receives only a rounded location on Android) or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can turn off Health, location and notification access in your phone’s settings at any time. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Manage account. Your photos are deleted first, then your account, challenges, logs and notes are removed from our live database immediately, and from backups within 30 days. If any part of the deletion fails, nothing is removed and the app tells you to try again.

Can’t sign in? See Delete your Steadfast account to ask us to delete it by email.

Children

Steadfast is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app. Questions: support@sarvlabs.app.
