Morning

A sky that knows your day.

The Today screen follows the real time and weather where you are. A clear morning looks like a clear morning.

Golden hour

Your day, as a wave.

Every task sits along a gentle timeline at the time you planned it. Check them off as you go.

Night

Night falls. The day is kept.

Finish the list and the day turns gold on your Journey. Tomorrow starts fresh.

Weather-aware

Rain at 3:55? It already knows.

When an outdoor workout lands in a wet slot, Steadfast checks the forecast and offers to wait for the dry spell. Or you go anyway. It suggests; you decide.

Journey

Seventy-five days. One road.

Every day you keep becomes a gold coin on the road. The milestones at 25, 50 and 75 are always in view.

Day 1 of 7574 days to go

Every evening

A quiet look back.

The day in review lists what you did and when. Add how it felt if you like. It never changes whether the day counted.

Hard days happen. Skips are forgiven.

When a challenge allows skips, Steadfast applies them for you and the next morning tells you exactly what was forgiven. No shame, no panic about streaks.
