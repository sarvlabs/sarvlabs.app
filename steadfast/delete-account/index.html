Account

Delete your Steadfast account

You can delete your Steadfast account and everything in it whenever you like, right from the app. If you can’t sign in, we’ll do it for you.

In the app

Open Steadfast and go to:

  1. Settings
  2. Account
  3. Delete account

Confirm, and your account is deleted.

What gets deleted

Immediately

Your account, challenges, photos, notes and feedback are removed from our live database.

Within 30 days

They are also removed from our backups.

If you can’t sign in

Email us from the address on your Steadfast account and ask us to delete it. We’ll delete the account within 7 days and let you know when it’s done.

Email us to delete support@sarvlabs.app

Have a subscription? Deleting your account doesn’t cancel it. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play as well. More in our terms and privacy policy.
