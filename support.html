Common questions

What happens if I miss a day?

It depends on the challenge. Strict challenges start again from Day 1. Others include forgiven skips, which Steadfast applies for you; the next morning’s review tells you what was forgiven. Some challenges simply note the miss and carry on.

How do I delete my account?

In Steadfast, go to You → Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account, challenges, logs, notes, photos, feedback and screenshots are removed right away, and from backups within 30 days. Can’t sign in? See how to delete by email

Does Steadfast share my Apple Health data?

No. Only if you turn it on, Steadfast reads steps, workouts, sleep and mindful minutes from Apple Health (or Health Connect on Android). It uses them only to offer them for the tasks you chose, and only when you tap. It never writes to Health, and it never shares or sells your health data or uses it for ads.
